Edward: "Go over everything in the ideas and see what we have remaining in the ways of low hanging
fruit or easy wins, anything that doesn't require a new manifest version." Three readers took a
third of docs/IDEAS.md each; every idea kept below was checked against the code as not built
since it was written. "A day" is a working day's change with its test. Nothing here changes what a
`.sieve` holds or how one is read (a packer choice that writes the same format is allowed).
