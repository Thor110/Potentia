## Looked at and left

  after the training check, which returns first; it does not happen.

  said in its help ("everything is read into memory").

## Optimisation

A file LZMA2 cannot shrink (sound, film, anything compressed already) spent most of its packing on
one LZMA2 pass at 9e over the whole file, for a figure the other ways then beat: the FLAC of 52 MB,
29.1 s of its 34 s. LZMA2 is at its slowest on such data (preset 6 was no faster, 26.3 s). Now a file
of 16 MiB or more is sampled first, 1 MiB at its start, middle and end at preset 6; where every
sample saves under 2%, the least-shrunk sample stands for the whole and the pass is not made. (The
average of the three was tried first: it put the figure 0.8% under the pass, so LZMA2 was taken
over the rope and came out 151,500 bytes larger; the least-shrunk sample does not.)

| | Before | After |
| :--- | ---: | ---: |
| The FLAC, 51,997,326 bytes, default | 33.6 s, 51,783,003 bytes | 11.6 s, 51,783,003 bytes (the same) |
| samba, Optimise Search | 3,277,403 bytes | 3,277,403 bytes (the same) |
| mozilla, Optimise Search | 13,376,239 bytes | 13,376,239 bytes (the same) |
| The FLAC, Optimise Search | | 428.4 s, 51,783,003 bytes |

With Optimise Search the FLAC is not helped: its time goes in the mixing passes the search makes,
not in the LZMA2 figure.

### Tried and taken back

Mixing a file over 16 MiB foretold from its start: the start was packed alone, its size scaled to
the whole, and where that could not clear the rising bar against the best way so far, the whole was
not mixed. Measured with Optimise Search:

| File | Bytes, before | Bytes, foretold | Time, before | Time, foretold |
| :--- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| The FLAC | 51,783,003 | 51,783,003 | 428.4 s | 427.0 s |
| mozilla | 13,376,239 | 13,376,239 | 165.7 s | 144.2 s |
| samba | 3,277,403 | 3,728,015 | 68.1 s | 49.3 s |
| enwik8 | 21,431,896 | 21,431,896 | 467 s | 566 s |

samba came out 13.7% larger (its start does not show how well the whole mixes), the FLAC gained
nothing and enwik8 was slower, so it was reverted; samba packs to 3,277,403 bytes again.
