## MP3: worth it, licence fits, not built: its decoder is not safe on hostile input

**Licence.** packMP3 (Matthias Stirner, github.com/packjpg/packMP3, last commit e61c119, 27 April 2020)
is LGPL v3 since version 1.0f; Sieve is AGPL v3, with which LGPL v3 code may be combined (it may be
taken under the GPL v3, which AGPL v3 §13 allows to be linked). So the licence fits.

**Measured.** Five MP3s made here by LAME (through ffmpeg) from recordings used before, each given back
byte for byte by packMP3 (in memory, its library's calls), about 0.4 s a megabyte each way:

| MP3 | Bytes | Sieve now (Balanced, Optimise Search) | packMP3 | |
| :--- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| jazz, 192 kbit/s | 1,090,290 | 1,086,841 | 971,994 | −10.6% |
| jazz, VBR (V2) | 883,756 | 881,743 | 785,803 | −10.9% |
| WoWRevived's Martian 03, 192 kbit/s | 1,090,290 | 1,082,252 | 952,746 | −12.0% |
| orchestra, 192 kbit/s | 668,205 | 664,037 | 568,600 | −14.4% |
| orchestra, VBR (V2) | 328,293 | 323,752 | 279,102 | −13.8% |

**Why it is not built.** On installing, the reader would decode packMP3's form of an MP3 from whatever
a `.sieve` holds. Fuzzed (each of 300 runs a packMP3 stream with 1 to 8 bytes changed, or cut short,
decoded under AddressSanitizer and UBSan): 264 of the 300 crashed: global buffer overflows in its
Huffman writer (huffmp3.cpp, line 258), member calls on null pointers in its arithmetic coder
(aricoder.cpp, line 391). Its coder also calls `exit(0)` when an allocation fails. A crafted `.sieve`
could so corrupt the installer's memory. Building it would first need packMP3's decoder made safe
(bounds on every table and length it reads; about 8,100 lines of C++), or run apart from the installer
where a crash does no harm. Left for Edward to decide.

## DDS: uncompressed built (shape from its header); compressed measured

**Built.** `header_shape` reads an uncompressed DDS (RGB or luminance, 8 to 32 bits, no FourCC, its
pitch the width's bytes): its width and bytes a pixel, for mixing (no format change). ImageMagick
6.9.12's DDS of the 800 by 450 door photograph: 206,692 bytes (no shape found), now 189,200 (−8.5%);
a 1920 by 1080 screenshot, whose shape was guessed right, unchanged (156,605); both installed byte for
byte.

**Measured, not built.** BC1 (DXT1) textures: mixing already finds their blocks' rows (the door,
180,928 bytes, carried in 42,761; the screenshot, 1,036,928, in 95,470). Splitting each block's colours
from its indices was not tried.

## ICO and CUR: already carried well

ImageMagick's icon of five sizes (16 to 256, 353,118 bytes) holds them as bitmaps, which mixing already
takes as pictures 256 wide (69,217 bytes, with and without the draft). PNGs inside icons are opened by
the zlib scan where their image is one IDAT (results/zlib-pcx-2026-11-08.md).
