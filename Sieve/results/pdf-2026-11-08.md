# PDFs opened: their deflate streams and their JPEGs (v34 draft, 8 November 2026)

Edward (8 November 2026): "make a start on listing out and implementing all formats across the board
that we can". The first of docs/FORMATS-PLAN.md.

**Built.** `pdf_skeleton` (tools/cli/pack.cpp): a PDF as a skeleton and members, as v31 carries a tar
(`skm`, which every reader since v31 reads): its text in the skeleton as it is; each stream filtered
by `/FlateDecode` alone (128 bytes of deflate or more) made again by preflate from what it holds, a
member; each by `/DCTDecode` alone with its `/Length` written as a number, a JPEG, a member as it is.
Other filters (JBIG2, JPX, LZW, chains of filters) stay in the skeleton. Opened where the skeleton,
what the deflate streams hold (LZMA2 or mixing) and the JPEGs (each at its brunsli form's size) come
out clearly under the PDF as it is (64 bytes and 0.5%), and checked to give the PDF back first. And,
the one change to what a reader reads (v34): a member of a skeleton (a PDF's, a zip's, a tar's) may
be a JPEG carried as its brunsli form (`jbr`), as a member of a text's spans already may.

**What it costs.** Each opened stream is a member searched as any file is: on arXiv 1706.03762, 47.4
of the 67.9 s went to the rope's search on its 117 members (one of 27.1 s). The command line has no
limit on a file's search unless `--search-per-file` gives one; the File Locator's time for a file
bounds it.

**The oracle** reads the listing and every member, and, having no preflate, says the PDF is not made
again by it (as of a zip's deflate). CI step 11 writes a PDF of eight deflated pages and a JPEG, and
checks it through Sieve and sieve-install.
