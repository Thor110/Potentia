# MP3 through packMP3, its decoder made safe for damaged input (v34 draft, 8 November 2026)

Edward (8 November 2026): "lets focus on making the decoder safe for MP3 then we can move on to the
other formats". Earlier the same day (results/mp3-dds-ico-2026-11-08.md): packMP3 made five MP3s 10.6%
to 14.4% smaller and its licence (LGPL v3) fits Sieve's AGPL v3, but its decoder crashed on 264 of 300
damaged streams, so it was not built.

## What was unsafe

packMP3's decoder says of itself "no error checks needed! (we already did this when compressing)": it
trusted every value it read. Found by fuzzing (libFuzzer, coverage-guided, with AddressSanitizer and
UndefinedBehaviorSanitizer, clang 18) and by reading the code:

- **The arithmetic coder** read outside its tables on a damaged stream: an escape from the table of
  order -1 (which holds every symbol) stepped below it; a count past the scale walked off the totals;
  a context past the model's indexed past its links. Reading past the stream's end returned zeros
  forever, so loops that decode until an end symbol (ID3 data) never ended. A failed allocation ended
  the program (`exit(0)`).
- **The side info** decoded from the stream (region tables, region bounds, block types, the small
  values' bound) was used unchecked to index models and Huffman tables: a region table of 4 or 14 has
  no model (a null pointer); a region bound past 576 ran off the 580-byte arrays; region sizes over 20
  bands indexed past the band table.
- **The frames' sizes**: a VBR frame's decoded bitrate, its main data's size (the index of a 2,049-entry
  table) and its padding and ancillary bits (written into 2,048-byte buffers) were not bounded.
- **The header's count of frames** (allocated before anything is decoded) was not bounded.

## What was changed

packMP3's source is kept in `third_party/packmp3` (its last commit, e61c119), each change marked
"Sieve" and listed in `third_party/packmp3/SIEVE-CHANGES.md`; its coding is unchanged. Each of the
above is checked against what an MPEG-1 Layer III frame may hold, and a damaged stream is refused: the
coder throws, the decoder returns its error, and Sieve's glue (`tools/cli/mp3_glue.cpp`, which holds a
lock, packMP3 keeping its state in globals) reports the MP3 as not made again; the install stops.

**Valid files are unchanged**: 17 MP3s (five full-length ones, twelve short ones: CBR and VBR, mono and
stereo, 32, 44.1 and 48 kHz, with and without ID3 tags) are coded to the same forms and given back byte
for byte, as before the changes. (An MPEG-2 file is refused at packing, as packMP3 always refused it.)

## Limits

A damaged stream can still ask for up to 2^20 frames, each decoded before the error that ends it is
met: installing such a file takes time (seconds to minutes) but reads and writes nothing outside its
buffers. The MPEG-2 and 2.5 MP3s packMP3 does not code are carried as their bytes.
