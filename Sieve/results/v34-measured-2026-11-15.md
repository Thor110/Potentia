# v34 measured (15 November 2026)

v34 was frozen on 12 November 2026 (docs/NEXT-VERSION.md); this is it measured whole against v33, as
v33 was on 3 November (results/v33-measured-2026-11-03.md), for list F3. Measured on a Linux machine
of 4 logical processors and 15 GB of memory. The sizes are the same on any machine; the times are this
machine's, and on it they wander: the same bytes made twice differed by up to about a fifth (mr, made
for size: 49.7 s once, 61.9 s the other), so a time is only worth reading where it differs by more.

## The run

`tools/bench/bench_files.py`, given `--also` for this (`--also=--v33` makes v33 byte for byte as before
the freeze, so each file is made both ways by the same build), two ways each, as the light bench makes
them: for size with Try every way (`size/try`) and for decoding without it (`decode/no`). Every
`.sieve` was installed again and its file checked against the original: **all 188 came back the same.**
Two folders, outside the repository:

- **The standard benchmark:** Silesia's twelve files (from its mirror on GitHub; the same bytes), enwik8,
  Don Quixote (`data/anchors/public-domain/pg996.txt`) and `control.py`
  (`data/anchors/ai-generated-content/gemini/`), as v33 was measured on. No box ticked.
- **v34's own kinds:** the six GIFs the GIF route was measured on (results/gif-opened-2026-11-06.md);
  a sound of 3 s as CI step 8 makes it (a 440 Hz tone and quiet noise, ffmpeg) as a 16-bit and a 24-bit
  WAV, and that sound as a 192 kbit/s MP3 (ffmpeg's LAME); one frame of a GIF saved by Pillow as a TIFF
  with LZW, a TIFF with PackBits, a PCX and a BMP; and three programs of this machine (`ctest`, 13.3 MB;
  `python3`, 6.5 MB; `ls`, 142 KB). Made twice: with no box, and with Compressed formats temporary
  (the GIF, TIFF and PCX routes make a file again on installing, so they work only with that box).

What it says:

- **On text and Silesia, v34 is v33** but for the one program in Silesia: every other file is the same
  bytes both ways (v34's new ways are for sound, pictures and programs). `ooffice`, a Windows DLL, is
  6.6% smaller made for size (1,741,263 against 1,864,843), by mix-v4's `E1`, the x86 instruction
  model, and decodes in 9.9 s against 8.0 s. Made for decoding, it is the same (LZMA2 with the x86
  filter both ways).
- **On v34's kinds**, with Compressed formats temporary, a third smaller made for size and a quarter
  made for decoding:
  - the GIFs, by far the most: 0.33 to 0.57 of v33 made for size, 0.40 to 0.69 made for decoding. They
    cost the most time too: about 80 to 140 s each to make for size against 17 to 31 s, and 24 to 44 s
    for decoding against 2 to 4 s (each frame's LZW is coded again and checked); and where their pixels
    are mixed, 11 to 17 s to decode against a tenth of a second (one, `rectangle`, chose LZMA2 even for
    size, decoding in 0.23 s);
  - the TIFFs: LZW 0.30 of v33 (Pillow's LZW, made again exactly), PackBits 0.74; the PCX 0.66 made for
    size, 0.97 for decoding; the BMP the same (v33 already read its pixels);
  - sound with no box needed: the 24-bit WAV 0.85 of v33 both ways, the 16-bit 0.92 and 0.94, each made
    and decoded faster than v33 (FLAC in place of mixing: 0.4 s to make for decoding against 5.5 and
    7.4); the MP3 0.89 both ways (packMP3), decoding in 0.025 s against 0.006;
  - programs: `ctest` 0.92 made for size and `ls` 0.93, by `E1`, decoding about a third slower
    (`ctest` 15.1 s to 22.1 s with the box, 15.2 s to 18.3 s without; times wander, as above); made for
    decoding, the same both ways.
- **`python3` came out the same both ways.** Sieve opens it as a container (something inside it is
  made again on installing, and 15 KB of it is text carried with what it decodes to), and the 6.5 MB
  it then mixes is mixed without `E1` (`B16 R1 M4 C2`). Whether `E1` would pay on it, or should be
  weighed on a program carried that way, was not measured here: a question for v35's list.
