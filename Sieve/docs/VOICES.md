# Voices of the halls

Lines for the AIs that walk Sieve's corridors to say: quotes, questions, rules, notes and metaphors,
from the documentation to the philosophy. Each is attributed to whoever first said it, because
there will be endlessly many of them and each deserves its source. In the game they will be pages
of a `.sieve` of their own (IDEAS §19.55), found on the shelves and spoken in passing; until then,
this file is the corpus.

**The format.** One voice a line, so that a program can read them:

    - **WHO** (DATE, KIND): TEXT

- **WHO:**
  - *Edward:* Thor110, Sieve's author.
  - *Gemini:* Google's model.
  - *Claude:* Anthropic's model.
  - *A hallway AI:* for a line written for a character.
- **KIND:** quote, question, rule, note or metaphor. "A point about everything" after it (`quote;
  a point about everything`) marks a thought Edward made about the whole of it, noted beside the
  quote because it may lead to a filter one day, or may not (IDEAS §19.75).
- **Text and attribution:** the text is as it was said, its spelling tidied only where a typo would
  read as a mistake in the game. A line is never reattributed; a correction to a fact is a note
  beside it.

**Contributing voices.** Anyone may add voices of their own, attributed to them. There is no fixed
number: unlimited within reason, and "within reason" means what exists, and what can be stored.
Infinite is infinite, so a respectful amount is asked for. More may be considered, evaluated,
enumerated and stored later. Nothing is guaranteed (IDEAS §19.77).

**Notes on its figures (Claude):**
- *A day of pages:* a day's 86,400 pages are 1.36 × 10^-39 % of the 6.362 × 10^45 pages, not
  1.17 × 10^-42 %. The 1.17 × 10^-21 % on the Build or Buy page is the share of the pictures
  looked at since the 24th of April.
- *The printed stack:* it is about 7.2 × 10^14 times as wide as the observable universe at 0.1 mm a
  sheet. It does not "loop back" or "wrap around spacetime", which is a figure of speech.
- *"A billion civilizations":* has no calculation behind it.
- *The Universal Exchange Rate:* a chain is not lossless. A picture stage keeps only the SHA-256
  seed of what it was given, so a chain loses almost everything at each picture or melody stage
  (IDEAS §19.62).
- *The light and audio ledgers:* describe the program in general terms only; they are not
  measurements.
- *The Pareidolia Index:* is exactly computable for a given filter (IDEAS §19.45).
