Edward (12 November 2026): "speech section for voices.md, larger lines, paragraphs and the like for
recording, maybe have a speech.md document actually to record and catalog speeches like the
following for models to speak in ways at chosen moments or during chosen paths through the state
space as per the procedural nature of the rules of the game itself which will naturally form from
the convergence of all state spaces to rule the topological nature of its landscape" (IDEAS §19.169).

[VOICES.md](VOICES.md) holds single lines, said in passing. This file holds the longer pieces: whole
paragraphs, kept as they were said (spelling tidied, nothing reworded), for the game's speakers to
give at a chosen moment or on a chosen path through the state space. Each speech is attributed and
dated, and says when it might be spoken. That cue is a suggestion until the game's rules decide it.

**The format.** Each speech has a heading, then one line of fields, then the speech as a quoted block:

- `who`: who said it first.
- `date`: when it was said.
- `kind`: *speech* (to be spoken whole) or *passage* (to be read, or spoken in parts).
- `when`: the moment or path it suits, such as *on entering Personal Dimensions*, *at the far end of
  a line*, or *after a long walk*.

A program can then read them as it reads VOICES.
