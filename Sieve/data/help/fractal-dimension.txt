index: fractal dimension, fractals, FRACTALS line, rulesets, Fibonacci word, golden ratio, L-system, substitution, Koch, dragon curve, index book

The fractal dimension is a line of the hallway, its door after AI, in light purple. Its books are the fractals charted by hand, each named with its ruleset; its first book is the index of them all. (Every ruleset of its grammar is on a line too, the text line with the format-fractal-v1 filter ticked; the shelves hold the charted ones.)

## Walking it
Go left through AI's far door, or choose FRACTALS as the line to start on. Hover over a book to see its name; take it with {key1:hallway.anywhere/take|E}, as any item. Warp ({key1:hallway.anywhere/warp|T}) to a fractal by its id, such as dragon-curve, or "index" for the first book.

## A fractal in hand
It shows what it is, its picture at the depth you hold it at, and its ruleset. {key1:hallway.holding/next_page|N} or {key1:hallway.anywhere/zoom_in|=} grows it a step deeper; {key1:hallway.holding/previous_page|B} or {key1:hallway.anywhere/zoom_out|-} takes it a step back. {key1:hallway.holding/view|Z} opens it in the viewer, large, where N and B step it too; {key1:hallway.holding/save|F} saves its ruleset, the steps that make it (the picture is only how it is shown); {key1:hallway.holding/favourite|H} keeps it as a favourite.

## The two kinds of ruleset
- A substitution: at every step each symbol becomes its word (0 becomes 01, 1 becomes 0, for the Fibonacci word). Drawn as its words one under another, each stretched across, so the pattern is seen repeating at every scale.
- An L-system: drawn as a turtle draws. F and G draw a line forward, f moves without one, + and - turn by its angle, [ and ] keep a place and come back to it.

## The shelves
The index, then the Fibonacci word (the golden ratio in two symbols), the Thue-Morse sequence, the Cantor set, the Koch curve and snowflake, Sierpinski's triangle, the dragon curve, the Hilbert, Levy C and Gosper curves, and a fractal plant. More are charted by adding them to fractals/chart.txt beside the program, each as its own .fractal file.

## On the command line
sieve fractal lists the shelves; sieve fractal show and draw grow one to a depth and draw it as a picture. See [[command-line|the command line]].
