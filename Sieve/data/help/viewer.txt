The item page draws what you hold at the page's size, so a long page stops at its foot. The viewer opens it over the whole window instead, as large as you like and scrolled both ways.

## Opening and closing
Holding something, press {key1:hallway.holding/view|Z}, or click the thing itself. Esc or Z closes the viewer, with the thing still in your hands.

## What it shows
- A page: all of it, a character to a cell.
- A picture or a film: its pixels, one to one or as large as you like. A film plays; Space stops it, and N and B step a frame.
- A book: the open page, with N and B to turn the pages. A track: its notes.
- A model or a world: in 3D, turned with the arrow keys or WASD, with its .obj text as the next tab.

## Files open by their kind
A file from the binary line opens in the view made for its kind, read from its own first bytes, with its bytes as the next tab:
- Pictures (PNG, JPEG, GIF and BMP) as the picture. A GIF's frames play.
- Text files as their text.
- Sound files (WAV, FLAC, AIFF and a few others) as their waveform, and MIDI files as their notes. Space plays either.
- Models (.obj, glTF and .glb) in 3D.

A kind with no viewer yet, MP3 and Ogg among them, is shown as its bytes. The viewer says so, and the kind is noted in viewers-needed.txt beside the settings file: the list of viewers still to be made.

## The buttons along the top
Tab and Shift+Tab, or the buttons, show what else was made of the thing on its way to the shelf: PICTURE, the picture on the item; COVER, a book's, track's or film's cover; and TITLE, its title. F saves what is shown.

## Moving about
- The wheel scrolls down, Shift and the wheel across, and Ctrl and the wheel zooms about the pointer. Dragging moves it too.
- The arrows or WASD move it; PgUp and PgDn by a screen; Home and End to the top and the foot.
- + and - zoom (with Shift, twice as far), 0 fits it to the window and 1 is one to one.
