Sieve can record what you do in the hallway and play it back exactly, step for step.

## Recording
Press F12 in the hallway to start a recording, and F12 again to stop it. Everything you do is kept, from where you stand: each key, the mouse, and the time of each frame. The recording is saved in a replays folder beside the settings file, packed as a .sieve file and named by when it began.

A recording stops by itself after a set time, or when the drive it is written to runs short of space. Either way it keeps what it has. Both limits are in the settings file.

## Playing one back
Choose Replays on the main menu. The few words at the top of the screen say how to record one, and below them every recording is listed.
- Enter plays the chosen recording in the hallway, from where it began, and then hands the hallway back to you.
- Space ticks its cache box, so a long recording is decoded once into the cache instead of each time it is played.

## When a recording will not play
A recording plays back exactly where the hallway starts as it did when it was made, with the same settings. One made elsewhere is refused, and Sieve says where it began.

## Good to know
F12 cannot be changed on the Keys screen yet. A recording can also be made or played from the command line: see [[command-line]].
