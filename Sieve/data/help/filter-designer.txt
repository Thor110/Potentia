The Filter Designer makes your own filters, as nodes, and tests them as you go. Open it from the main menu or the pause menu.

## What a filter is made of
A filter you design has:
- the filters it requires, each with the settings it pins (ticking yours ticks them too),
- its header and parameters,
- its rule: sets of words joined by arrows that say which may follow which, or a table.

## Testing as you go
As you build, the Designer tests the filter and shows:
- how many units of the line survive it,
- samples of what it keeps,
- text you type, judged, with where it fails,
- any other filter that is the same rule under another name.

On the audio line the samples are played: the first at each test, and the next with P.

## Saving
The Designer saves each filter as a version. It is kept in the filters folder as a .sieve file, together with the word lists it reads, and checked before it is kept.

## Using your filter
Your filters appear in their own CUSTOM FILTERS tab of a line's filter list in the setup menu. They tick and take settings like the built-in filters. See [[filters]].

## Melody filters
In the [[media-player]], {key1:media/melody|M} opens the Designer on a melody filter to change as you like. Each time it is tested it plays a melody it keeps, and Esc goes back to the player.

The Designer's own keys cannot be changed yet.
