The hallway opens on the main menu, Potentia : Sieve : Gallery of Babel Dimensions. Use {key1:menus/up|Up} and {key1:menus/down|Down} to choose a row and {key1:menus/choose|Enter} to open it, or use the mouse. {key1:menus/back|Esc} goes back one screen.

## The rows
- Start Sieve: set up the lines, then walk into the hallway. See [[first-walk]].
- File Locator: weigh a file or a folder beside zip and 7z, and make Sieve instructions and installers. See [[file-locator]].
- Media Player: the background music, its settings, filters, recent melodies and favourites. See [[media-player]].
- Filter Designer: make a filter as nodes, test it as you go and save it. See [[filter-designer]].
- AI Training Harness: train a model of the AI line from a recorded run. See [[training-harness]].
- Personal Dimensions: your own dimension, other people's, and the ones made together. See [[personal-dimension]].
- Character Creator: your character, large, with its settings and actions. See [[character-creator]].
- Replays: your recordings of the hallway, to play back. See [[replays]].
- Encryption Engine: encrypt with a recipe of layers, decrypt, or try to break a weak cipher. See [[encryption-engine]].
- Manual Navigation: turn a file's bytes by hand and see where each turn puts it. See [[manual-navigation]].
- System Viewer: the systems charted so far, walked as a ring of doors. See [[system-viewer]].
- Documentation: this library, the pages you are reading, with its contents, index and search.
- Potentia Settings: graphics, controls, language, the programs Sieve runs, and more. See [[settings]].
- Exit Sieve: closes Sieve.

## Good to know
Pressing {key1:menus/back|Esc} on the main menu itself points at Exit Sieve rather than closing Sieve, so you will not leave by accident.

The Media Player builds the hallway with the player open over it. Leaving the player brings you back to the main menu.

Many of these systems can also be opened while you walk, from the pause menu. See [[pause-menu]].
