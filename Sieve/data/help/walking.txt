## Moving
- {key1:hallway.walking/forward|W} walks forward and {key1:hallway.walking/back|S} back.
- {key1:hallway.walking/left|A} and {key1:hallway.walking/right|D} step to the side.
- {key1:hallway.walking/turn_left|Left} and {key1:hallway.walking/turn_right|Right} turn.
- Hold {key1:hallway.walking/run|Left Shift} to run.

The mouse looks around. {key1:hallway.anywhere/mouse|Tab} frees the mouse or captures it again.

## Going further, faster
The corridor goes on for ever, so walking is not the only way along it.
- The mouse wheel jumps one tile of corridor at a time. Spun quickly it goes further, and a pause or a turn back starts it at one tile again.
- {key1:hallway.anywhere/thousand_on|PgUp} and {key1:hallway.anywhere/thousand_back|PgDn} jump a thousand tiles.
- With empty hands, {key1:hallway.empty_handed/million_on|]} and {key1:hallway.empty_handed/million_back|[} jump a million tiles.
- {key1:hallway.anywhere/home|Home} takes you to tile 0, the start of every line, marked by a double start line on the floor.

To jump straight to something particular, see [[warp-and-addresses]].

## Where you are
Each line is a loop. Where each copy of a line begins, a checkered start line crosses the floor. In a corner of the screen a compass draws the corridor as rings, one for each line, with a needle at your place on the line you are on and a mark on every other ring where you last stood on that line. Zero is at the top, and it is the same place for every line.

## Doors
The walls repeat shelf, door, shelf. A sign over each doorway names the line it leads to, in that line's colours. A door in the left wall leads to the next line and one in the right wall to the previous line. A door keeps your place in the corridor: step straight back through it and you are exactly where you were. See [[lines]].

## Other keys
- {key1:hallway.anywhere/ordering|M} switches the ordering: positional, scrambled, then guided on the pages line.
- {key1:hallway.anywhere/menu|F1} opens the setup menu over the hallway.
- Esc opens the [[pause-menu]], closes a panel, or frees the mouse.
- {key1:hallway.anywhere/quit|Ctrl+Q} quits.

Every key here can be changed: see [[keys]].
