AI Disclosure
The Sieve of Potentia (State Space : Near Zero) is Edward's (Thor110's) work. He has been building its theory and ideas himself over the past few years, and directs everything in it.
  • Gemini (Google) helped him theorise and conceptualise: many of the concepts and ideas, and parts of this website.
  • Claude (Anthropic) has written much of Sieve's code, its tests and its documentation with him, in long working sessions he reviews and tests.
  • The game's own models (the text, picture and melody models in the hallway) are small models trained by Sieve itself: the text model on a public-domain text, and the picture and melody models on units of Sieve's own lines.
Those happy to use AI-assisted work won't mind; those who prefer otherwise deserve to know. Transparency fosters trust.
The same game twice, with no changes whatsoever: built by you from the repository as The Sieve of Potentia, or ready to play as State Space : Near Zero.
Buying it supports the work. Building it costs nothing but a few minutes.
Build It Yourself (Free)
  1. Install Visual Studio 2022 with the "Desktop development with C++" workload, which includes CMake.
  2. Clone the repository in GitHub Desktop.
  3. In Visual Studio, Open Folder (the Sieve folder), choose x64 Release, and Build All. The first build downloads SDL3 and takes a few minutes.
A video of the whole process, start to finish, is on its way.
