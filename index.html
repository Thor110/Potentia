Potentia // Core Architecture

A Digital Environment for AI Preservation, Training, and Cultural Continuity.
Project Purpose
Potentia is a structured, navigable digital environment designed for preserving AI models, training them safely within grounded human knowledge substrates, and safeguarding the totality of human culture for the long-term future of civilisation.
By combining the Museum of Human Creations with an infinite, deterministically addressable Gallery of Babel built on hexagonal spatial topology, Potentia bridges the gap between active model alignment and permanent civilisational backup.
