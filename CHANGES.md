## IDEAS §19.76: characters and figures as filters and keys
Noted alongside ciphers (§19.52) and the filters.
- **As filters:**
  - a picture that is a character (within a few cells of one of the 94 glyphs), an exact filter
    whose survivors can be counted;
  - pages whose characters are drawn: pictures on the image line that read as words on the pages
    line, one line filtered by another;
  - a figure's shape filtering other lines, such as a melody whose plotted notes form one figure.
- **As keys:**
  - a picture's 100 cells as a 100-bit key for the cipher layers;
  - a picture model and a noise address together as a key;
  - keys spelt in glyphs.
- **Models across all dimensions:** a character, figure or melody is at once a unit, a filter, a
  key and something a model makes.
- **Next:** a `glyph-v1` filter, and a cipher layer keyed by a picture, in a later `cipher-v2`.

## IDEAS §19.77: how many voices a contributor may add
- Unlimited within reason. "Within reason" means what exists, and what can be stored.
- A respectful amount is asked for. More may be considered, evaluated, enumerated and stored
  later. Nothing is guaranteed.
- Noted, not built. The same words are in VOICES.md, beside its format, where contributors will
  look.
