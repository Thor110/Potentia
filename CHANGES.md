# Start Sieve: each line's details (9 November 2026)

Three dots after each line's name in the map (as tall as the magnifying glass), or I, open a window
over the map, as the filters window opens, saying what that line's figures mean:
- Size: the formula and what it counts (title, covers, content), ~10^N units.
- Address: its length in bits and in decimal digits; the bars are drawn to it.
- Walking it: the years at one unit a second (the column's "~10^115y"), and how many times the age of
  the universe that is.
- Tiles of 128 and the empty slots in the last one.
- Filters ticked, the survivors they leave and the share removed.
- On this machine: time to open one unit against the time budget, and the unit cache its units take.
- Warnings: too large to open, or slow, with what to do.

I opens it for the line whose settings row is chosen (else the line you will start on), as F does
for filters. In the window: Left/Right step through the lines, F goes to that line's filters, Esc or
I closes it; a click outside closes it. The footer says "I or the dots: its details".

data/lang/en.txt, ci/steps/10-hallway.sh, docs/MANUAL.md. Holds the key-typing fix too (menu.cpp).
